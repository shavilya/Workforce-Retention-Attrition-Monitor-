# Workforce Retention & Attrition Monitor 

## Demo / How This Project Works
This Power BI dashboard provides comprehensive workforce presence analytics, tracking employee attendance patterns and work location preferences over time.

## Dashboard Overview
The dashboard displays the following key insights:

### Summary Metrics (April-June 2022): <br>
1)<b>Total Working Days</b>: 2,386 days (likely represents total possible working days across the entire team) <br>
2)<b>Present Days</b>: 4,064 days (cumulative attendance across all employees - multiple employees present on the same day adds to this count) <br>
3)<b>Average Presence Rate</b>: 1.70 (170% - this appears to be calculated differently, possibly representing total presence relative to a baseline) <br>
4)<b>Work From Home (WFH) Rate</b>: 11% of total attendance <br>
5)<b>Sick Leave (SL) Rate</b>: 2% of total attendance <br> 

## Key Observations:

Presence Trends: The "Presence % by Date" chart shows relatively stable attendance around 95-100% throughout April and May, with a significant drop in late June to approximately 50%
WFH Pattern Shift: Work-from-home percentage remained consistently low (under 10%) from April through early June, then dramatically spiked to nearly 100% in late June, indicating a major shift to remote work
Sick Leave Stability: Sick leave percentage remained low and stable (mostly under 5%) throughout the three-month period with minor daily variations
Individual Tracking: The employee table shows individual presence rates, with Zaiden Wheeler at 2.17 and Tori Shannon at 1.77, while the daily attendance grid tracks specific presence types (P = Present, HML, HPL) for each employee
